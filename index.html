Chapter 02

Silver on the tarmac.

A brief timeline of the three-pointed star at speed — six moments that carved the myth of Mercedes-Benz motorsport. A birthday is also a starting grid; here are the laps that came before yours.

01
1901

Enter the star

Emil Jellinek names a new Daimler race car after his daughter Mercédès. A three-pointed star is born — land, sea, sky.

02
1934

The Silver Arrow

Legend has it the W25 exceeded the 750kg limit by 1kg. Overnight, the white paint was scraped off — silver metal underneath, silver arrows forever.

03
1954

Fangio · W196

Juan Manuel Fangio returns to Mercedes and dominates the World Championship. The car with the inline-eight became the sound of an era.

04
1955

10:07:48 — Mille Miglia

Stirling Moss and Denis Jenkinson pilot the 300 SLR at an average of 157.65 km/h across 1000 miles of Italian roads. Untouchable.

05
2014–2021

Hybrid Dynasty

Mercedes-AMG Petronas wins 8 straight Constructors' Championships. Hamilton, Rosberg, Bottas, Russell — Brackley's finest hour.

06
Today

The Best or Nothing

From Maybach showrooms to pit lanes at Silverstone — the star still points to speed, craft, and quiet luxury.
